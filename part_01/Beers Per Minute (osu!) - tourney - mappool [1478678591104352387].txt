[05/03/2026 6:13 pm] hydrangeaze
nm1 - comfy aim
nm2 - comfy streams
nm3 - alt
nm4 - tech but sometimes very alty tech
nm5 - sometimes gimmick (low ar) if there is no nm6 but its supposed to be speed
nm6 - gimmick
hd1 - aim with a bit less comfortability than nm1 (could be awkward aim, a bit of tech, some alt, anything really to make it less comfy)
hd2 - low ar and perhaps awkward aim (gimmick)
hd3 - more of a tech pick than hd1, hd slider tech, alt, etc. can all be in this slot
hr1 - comfy aim
hr2 - cs 6.5 mostly
hr3 - tech or alt or really anything that isnt normal mechanics
dt1 - aim, doesnt have to be "high" bpm or ar9 but ig that depends on the tournament's skill level
dt2 - speed stamina, really not pooled well a lot of the time but usually it will be pure speed or finger control
dt3 - finger control, a lot of the time this and dt2 are basically indistinguishable but there is a difference, I swear
dt4 - gimmick dt, could be anything really but just not raw mechanics most of the time
fm1 - fm is a bit harder to generalize but this is usually more mechanics focused, similar to hd1 and hr1 (sometimes this is pooled as speed)
fm2 - this is most of the time ar8 cs5, similar to hd2 and hr2 (gimmick)
fm3 - usually comfortable stats (ar9+ and around cs4) but can basically be anything except, similar to hd3 and hr3 kinda usually maybe

fm4 - anything really
tb1 - long map, various skillsets but usually tests aim, alt, tech and stamina more than speed and gimmick skills.
