## 1. Error Calculations (Računi z napakami)

### Relative vs. Absolute Error

| Relative Error (Relativna napaka) | Absolute Error (Absolutna napaka) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Example:** $X = 2.1 \cdot (1 \pm \frac{0.2}{2.1})$ | **Example:** $X = 2.1 \, \text{m} \pm 0.2 \, \text{m}$ |
| Represents the error as a fraction of the average value. | Represents the direct uncertainty in the measurement. |

---

### Example Task: Calculating Average and Error

Given the following measurements:
- $X_1 = 102.3 \, \text{m}$
- $X_2 = 102.2 \, \text{m}$
- $X_3 = 102.4 \, \text{m}$
- $X_4 = 102.1 \, \text{m}$
- $X_5 = 102.2 \, \text{m}$
- $X_6 = 102.0 \, \text{m}$

**1. Calculate the average (povprečje):**
$$
\bar{X} = \frac{\sum X_i}{n}
$$
$$
\bar{X} = \frac{102.3 + 102.2 + 102.4 + 102.1 + 102.2 + 102.0}{6} \approx 102.2 \, \text{m}
$$

**2. Calculate the deviations (Δ):**
- $\Delta_1 = |102.3 - 102.2| = 0.1$
- $\Delta_2 = |102.2 - 102.2| = 0.0$
- $\Delta_3 = |102.4 - 102.2| = 0.2$
- $\Delta_4 = |102.1 - 102.2| = 0.1$
- $\Delta_5 = |102.2 - 102.2| = 0.0$
- $\Delta_6 = |102.0 - 102.2| = 0.2$

The final result is presented with the calculated average and the estimated error.

### Operations with Absolute Errors

**Addition:**
> When adding measurements, their absolute errors are added together.
$$
X_1 = 1.2 \, \text{m} \pm 0.1 \, \text{m}
$$
$$
X_2 = 1.4 \, \text{m} \pm 0.2 \, \text{m}
$$
$$
X = X_1 + X_2 = (1.2 + 1.4) \pm (0.1 + 0.2) = 2.6 \, \text{m} \pm 0.3 \, \text{m}
$$

**Subtraction:**
> Even when subtracting measurements, their absolute errors are **still added** together.
$$
X = X_1 - X_2 = (1.2 - 1.4) \pm (0.1 + 0.2) = -0.2 \, \text{m} \pm 0.3 \, \text{m}
$$

**Multiplication and Division:**
> For multiplication and division, we use and sum the **relative errors**.
$$
a = 5.1(1 \pm 0.3) \, \text{m}
$$
$$
b = 2.0(1 \pm 0.2) \, \text{m}
$$
$$
S = a \cdot b = (5.1 \cdot 2.0) \cdot (1 \pm (0.3 + 0.2)) = 10.0 (1 \pm 0.5) \, \text{m}^2
$$

### Pulleys (Škripci)

**Fixed Pulley (Obremen):**
The pulling force equals the gravitational force.
$$ \vec{F}_B = \vec{F}_g $$

```mermaid
graph TD
    A(Ceiling) -- Rope --> P(Pulley);
    P -- Rope --> W 1(Weight F_g ↓);
    P -- Rope --> F(Pull F_B ↓);

    style P fill: #f9f ,stroke: #333 ,stroke-width:2px
```


**Movable Pulley (Prosti, gibljivi):**
A system with a movable pulley.
$$ \sum \vec{F}_v = \vec{F}_g $$
$$ F_v + F_v = F_g \implies 2 F_v = F_g $$

```mermaid
graph TD
    subgraph System
        A(Support) -- Rope --> P 1(Fixed Pulley);
        P 1 -- Rope --> F(Pull F_v ↑);
        RopeEnd -- Rope --> P 2(Movable Pulley);
        A -- Rope --> RopeEnd;
        P 2 -- attached --> W(Weight F_g ↓);
        F -.-> P 2;
    end

    style P 1 fill: #f9f ,stroke: #333 ,stroke-width:2px
    style P 2 fill: #f9f ,stroke: #333 ,stroke-width:2px
```

## 4. Thermodynamics (Termodinamika)

**Specific Heat:**
The heat required to change the temperature of a substance.
$$
Q = m \cdot c \cdot \Delta T
$$
- For water: $c_{\text{voda}} = 4200 \, \text{J/kgK}$

**Latent Heat:**
The heat required to change the phase of a substance (e.g., melting or boiling).
$$
Q = m \cdot q
$$
- $q$: Specific latent heat of fusion (talilna) or vaporization (izparilna).
- Latent heat of fusion for water: $q_{t, H_2 O} = 336 \, \text{kJ/kg}$
