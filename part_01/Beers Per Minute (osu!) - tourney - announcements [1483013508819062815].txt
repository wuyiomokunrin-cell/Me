# We are hosting a tournament. 

We are collabing with **THSL** and inviting @everyone@here to a ***private open rank*** tournament between [**BPM**](https://osu.ppy.sh/teams/1306) team and members of [**Tech Slxts [THSL]**](https://osu.ppy.sh/teams/3057) team. 
-# BPM & THSL Collab Tournament

## Overview:
- **Teams of 4**: two of our members and two members of the other faction in each team.
- You don't get to pick your teammates; however, teams will be balanced based on everyone's performance in the Qualifiers to ensure fair matches.
- The number of available spots is **32 total, 16 for each clan**.
- **Prize pool**: As of the publication of this announcement, the prize pool consists of **1 month of osu! Supporter** for every member of the winning team. **However,** throughout the duration of the tournament, anyone can contribute additional funds to the prize pool via [**this PayPal account**](https://www.paypal.com/paypalme/BeersPerMinute) If you wish to donate money, I strongly suggest messaging one of the event organizers beforehand.
- You can invite anyone outside your team to participate in this tournament as a representative. However, their registration will only be accepted if the minimum number of players has **not** been reached! All players willing to participate must **also /link** with the @Bartender in order to get registered. 
- Registrations **can** be extended until the end of the qualifiers in case someone might have missed the opportunity. 
- Since this is a small and private tournament: **all players** from the clans are eligible to participate; **even the sponsors and tourney organizers**. 
- **Single elimination bracket**
- Difficulty ranging from 6.8★ to 7.4★ (Qualifiers to Finals)
-# This is our first tournament, so please be lenient. We are always open to suggestions.

# Qualifiers are @here!
@everyone, thank you for your patience. 
We are now officially moving into the Qualifiers stage of the BPM & THSL Collab Tournament.

## Qualifiers Mappool & Client:
The mappool for this stage is now finalized! You can find all the beatmaps and related information in the Excel spreadsheet posted in #tourney. The maps are final and won't be changed. 
The mappool consists of 4 NM maps, 3 HD maps, 3 HR maps and 3 DT maps. 
**Important:** This tournament is played on **osu!lazer**. Since lazer scoring is much more lenient than stable SV2, we expect some challenging maps. Failing a map doesn't necessarily mean a DQ, as the API will still pull your failed scores from the match history.

## Late Registrations:
We are postponing any ruleset changes and will still accept applications until the end of the Qualifiers stage. If you missed the initial deadline or know someone who still wants to participate as a representative, there is still time to secure a spot.
Late registrants must follow the same procedure: `/link` your account with the @Bartender bot and use the `/apply` command to get registered (or use the tourney register with ID 9).
-# note that the registrations will close when the qualifiers are concluded. 

## Scheduling & Lobbies:
- **Self-Scheduling**: Pick a date when you're comfortable playing for real and post it in the **#schedule** channel. 
- **Rescheduling**: Please commit to the schedule you set. If you become a "re-scheduling demon", the organizers will pick a date for you or ban you.
- **Lobby Requirements**: You can coordinate your lobby with **~~at least 3 registered players~~** yourself. 
- **One Sitting**: The entire qualifier **MUST** be played in one sitting. 
- **No Retries / Cheating**: You cannot play through the mappool twice! Our bot filters all unregistered players, tracks if runs are actually completed in one sitting, and catches anyone trying to play twice. Please note that while this is not an "official" tournament, any workarounds or cheating will result in a **ban without appeal**. 

## Referees & Submitting Results:
- Lobbies for the Qualifiers stage are entirely **self-refereed**. If you are acting as the referee, you will make the lobby, invite the players, and queue every map. 
- **No-shows:** If someone fails to show up on time, they'll be forced to schedule another lobby. Anyone who fails to submit a lobby or doesn't show up for the full qualifier is forced to surrender.
- **Submitting:** After you are done, send the lobby link to a referee. They will take a look and, if everything is in order, submit the lobby through the @Bartender bot. Alternatively, you can post the accessible mp link or HTML file (by saving with CTRL+S on the osu! events page) to @Rabusu.

## Team Formation:
For those wondering how teams will be formed from the qualifier results: 
Teams will be strictly randomized after the qualified players are announced. However, we will make sure to tweak and balance the teams to ensure no team gets unfair advantages from the random mix. 

## Content Creation:
Feel free to livestream your qualifier lobbies or record them to post on YouTube! It’s highly encouraged so we can have a record of the tournament to look back on, and so others can watch and learn. 

Time for schedules:
- You can start with the schedules and play them out immediately. 
- The qualifier lobbies are valid until including 29/03/2026 11:59 pm. 

**Prize pool:** as of my knowledge, the prize pool remains unchanged.

-# And a quick note (for the osu! officials):
-# This is NOT an OFFICIAL osu! event or tourney nor has this "tourney" intention to resemble one, this event is only for entertainment purposes only and rewards are enforced to ensure engagement between the servers.

***Thank you and good luck!***
*Team BPM and Team THSL*
