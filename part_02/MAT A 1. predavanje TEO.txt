## [[Izpitni Sistem in Ocenjevanje (Exam System and Grading)]]

The exam is worth a total of **100 possible points**, split into two main parts:
*   **Računski del (Computational Part):** 60 points (based on exercises/ `vaje`).
*   **Teoretični del (Theoretical Part):** 40 points (based on lectures/ `predavanja`).

There are two distinct pathways for completing the exam requirement:
1.  **Sprotni Testi (Continuous Testing):** The recommended path with extra benefits.
2.  **Klasični Izpitni Roki (Classic Exam Periods):** The standard path with no benefits.

### [[Pogoji za Pozitivno Oceno (Conditions for a Passing Grade)]]
To pass the course, you must meet **all** of the following criteria:
> *   **Skupaj vsaj 50 točk** (A total of at least 50 points).
> *   Od tega vsaj **15 točk** na teoretičnem delu.
> *   In vsaj **24 točk** na računskem delu.
> *   The remaining 11 points (to reach 50) can come from either part.

### [[Sprotni Testi (Continuous Testing Path)]]
This path is available only to students enrolled in the first year for the first time (not for those with `podaljšan status`). It offers significant advantages.
*   **Kvizi (Quizzes):** The average of your best 10 quizzes throughout the semester can add **up to 10 additional points** to your final theoretical score.
*   **Domače Naloge (Homework):** Successful completion of homework can add **up to 5 additional points** to your final computational score.

 #### **Primer Izračuna Ocene s Sprotnimi Testi (Example Grade Calculation with Continuous Testing)**
 This example from the lecture shows how the benefits work in practice.


|                    |     |     |     |                          |     |       |                    |                       |
| ------------------ | --- | --- | --- | ------------------------ | --- | ----- | ------------------ | --------------------- |
| Del (Part)         | 1.T | 2.T | S.T | $\Sigma$ (Povprečje)<br> | DN  | Kvizi | **Skupaj (Total)** | **Pogoj (Condition)** |
| **Računski del**   | 25  | 30  | 14  | **23**                   | 5   | /     | **28**             | $\ge 24 \checkmark$   |
| **Teoretični del** | 10  | 26  | 11  | **16**                   | /   | 7     | **23**             | $\ge 15 \checkmark$   |
|                    |     |     |     |                          |     |       | **51**             | $\ge 50 \checkmark$   |


 **Step-by-Step Analysis (The "Why"):**
 1.  **Average Test Scores ($\Sigma$):** The scores from the three tests (1.T, 2.T, S.T) are averaged.
> *   Računski: $(25 + 30 + 14) / 3 = 69 / 3 = 23$ points.
> *   Teoretični: $(10 + 26 + 11) / 3 = 47 / 3 \approx 15.67$, which is rounded to **16** points.
> 1.  **Adding Bonus Points:** The points from homework (DN) and quizzes are added.
> *   Računski: $23 (\text{tests}) + 5 (\text{DN}) = 28$ points.
> *   Teoretični: $16 (\text{tests}) + 7 (\text{Kvizi}) = 23$ points.
> 1.  **Final Check:** The total scores are checked against the minimum requirements.
> *   Računski total (28) is $\ge 24$. **Pass.**
> *   Teoretični total (23) is $\ge 15$. **Pass.**
> *   Overall total ($28 + 23 = 51$) is $\ge 50$. **Pass.**
> *   The student has successfully passed the exam.

### [[Klasičen Izpit (Classic Exam Path)]]
*   This path has **NOBENIH UGODNOSTI** (no benefits).
*   Points from quizzes and homework do not count. Your grade is based solely on your performance during the scheduled exam periods.
